Major Puja Festivals

Grand annual celebrations, deep legends, and sacred timing.

Sharadotsav Autumn (Sep - Oct)

Durga Puja

The grand celebration of Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura, symbolizing the triumph of divine righteousness over evil forces.

Key Rituals: Bodhon, Pushpanjali, Dhunuchi Naach, Sandhi Puja, Bijoya Dashami.
Traditional Bhog: Khichuri, Labra, Payesh, Sandesh.
Festival of Lights Kartik Amavasya

Diwali & Lakshmi Puja

Illuminating homes with rows of glowing diyas to welcome Goddess Lakshmi (Wealth & Prosperity) and Lord Ganesha into purified hearts and dwellings.

Key Rituals: House cleaning, Rangoli art, Diya lighting, Chopda Pujan, Kubera Aradhana.
Traditional Bhog: Besan Ladoo, Kheer, Kaju Katli, Panchamrit.
Vinayaka Utsav Bhadrapada (Aug - Sep)

Ganesh Chaturthi

Honoring Lord Ganesha—the remover of obstacles (Vighnaharta) and deity of wisdom, new beginnings, and prosperity.

Key Rituals: Pranapratishtha, 21 Durva grass offerings, Atharvashirsha recitation, Visarjan procession.
Traditional Bhog: Ukadiche Modak (steamed sweet dumplings), Motichoor Ladoo, Puran Poli.
Vasant Panchami Spring (Jan - Feb)

Saraswati Puja

Welcoming spring by worshipping Goddess Saraswati—the divine embodiment of knowledge, music, arts, wisdom, and speech.

Key Rituals: Wearing bright yellow attire, placing books and instruments at goddess feet, Hate Khori (child learning ceremony).
Traditional Bhog: Yellow Khichdi, Kesari Halwa, Plum chutney, Rajbhog.
Great Night of Shiva Phalguna (Feb - Mar)

Mahashivratri

The auspicious night commemorating Lord Shiva's cosmic dance (Tandava) and sacred union with Goddess Parvati.

Key Rituals: All-night vigil (Jagaran), Shiva Lingam Abhishekam with milk, honey, and Bilva (Bael) leaves, Om Namah Shivaya chanting.
Traditional Bhog: Thandai, Makhana Kheer, Sabudana Khichdi, Fruit offerings.
Solar & Divine Energy Kartik Shukla (Oct - Nov)

Chhath Puja & Kali Puja

Profound expressions of devotion offering gratitude to Surya Dev (Sun Deity) & Chhathi Maiyya, alongside midnight Kali Puja.

Key Rituals: 36-hour nirjala fast, sunset & sunrise river bank Arghya offerings, midnight Tantric Kali Aradhana.
Traditional Bhog: Thekua, Kheer, Sugarcane, Fresh Fruits, Red Hibiscus flower offerings.

Universal Illumination Rigveda

Gayatri Mantra

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः । तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं ।
भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि । धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Om Bhur Bhuvaḥ Swaḥ | Tat Savitur Vareṇyaṃ | Bhargo Devasya Dhīmahi | Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Pracodayāt ||

Meaning: We meditate on the radiant glory of the divine Creator (Savitur), who illuminated the three worlds. May That Supreme Light inspire and enlighten our intellect.

Removal of Obstacles Ganesha Stuti

Ganesh Vakratunda Sloka

वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥
Vakratunda Mahākāya Sūryakoṭi Samaprabha | Nirvighnaṃ Kuru Me Deva Sarvakāryeṣu Sarvadā ||

Meaning: O Lord with the curved trunk and immense form, possessing the radiance of ten million suns! Please clear all obstacles from all my endeavors, always.

Auspicious Divine Feminine Devi Mahatmyam

Durga Mangala Stuti

सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिबे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
Sarva Maṅgala Māṅgalye Śive Sarvārtha Sādhike | Śaraṇye Tryambake Gauri Nārāyaṇi Namo'stu Te ||

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Narayani, the auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, the fulfiller of every desire, the three-eyed refuge of all devotees.

Health & Spiritual Liberation Yajurveda

Mahamrityunjaya Mantra

ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात् ॥
Om Tryambakaṃ Yajāmahe Sugandhiṃ Puṣṭivardhanam | Urvārukamiva Bandhanān Mṛtyor Mukṣīya Mā'mṛtāt ||

Meaning: We worship the Three-Eyed Lord Shiva who is fragrant and nourishes all beings. As a cucumber is liberated from its vine, may He liberate us from death and bind us to immortality.
